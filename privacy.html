Privacy Policy

Sirkadiyen · Last updated: October 6, 2026

Sirkadiyen ("the app", "we") gives circadian timing suggestions. It works without accounts, ads or tracking.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for your real name, birth date, email address or phone number. There is no sign-up. We do not show ads and do not track you across other apps or websites.

Data stored only on your device

The emoji and optional nickname you choose, your age, biological sex, sleep need, wake time, chosen city or location, travel plans and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Apple Health (optional)

If you allow it, the app reads sleep, heart-rate variability and daylight data from Apple Health to compute your Rhythm score. This data is read on your device, is never sent to us or any third party, and is never used for advertising. The app does not write to Apple Health.

Location (optional)

If you allow it, your approximate location is used on your device to calculate local sunrise and sunset. It is not sent to us.

Calendar and notifications (optional)

Calendar access is used on your device only to avoid suggesting activities during busy events. Notifications are scheduled locally.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details. To check whether you have an active Sirkadiyen Pro subscription, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives a random anonymous identifier and your purchase history from Apple. This is used only to unlock Pro and restore purchases, and is not linked to your identity.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Your rights (GDPR / KVKK)

Because we do not hold data that identifies you, there is generally nothing for us to access or delete. You can delete all app data by deleting the app. For any request, contact us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
