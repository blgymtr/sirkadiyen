Sirkadiyen

Hormone clock & daily timing

Sirkadiyen shows which hormones peak at every hour of your day and the best time to focus, train, nap and sleep, tuned to your age. No account, no sign-up; your data stays on your device.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the subscription screen and tap Restore. Your subscription is tied to your Apple ID.

How do I cancel?

iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Sirkadiyen. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period.

Is this medical advice?

No. Hormone curves are a relative, modelled simulation based on published circadian research, not lab measurements. Consult a doctor before changing exercise, diet, sleep or medication.
